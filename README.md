# Asyntai – AI Chatbot for Moodle LMS

Add a 24/7 AI chatbot to your Moodle site that answers student questions instantly — login issues, grades, enrollment, assignments, course navigation, deadlines, policies. It answers from your own courses, policies, and documents, so answers are specific to your institution, not generic.

Built for universities, schools, and training organizations using Moodle.


## Why Asyntai for Moodle?

• **Instant answers, 24/7**: Students get help at night, on weekends, during exam periods — no waiting for IT or instructors.

• **Knows your Moodle site**: The AI answers from your courses, policies, FAQs, and uploaded content. Answers are specific to your institution.

• **Reduces support tickets**: Handles repetitive questions (password resets, login, deadlines, enrollment) so your staff can focus on real issues.

• **Works in every language**: Detects each student's language automatically and replies in it.

• **Stays on topic**: Only answers about your courses, policies, and procedures. Won't go off-script on random questions.

• **Fast (1–3s)**: Replies feel like a real conversation, not a slow form.

• **Easy to install**: Activate the plugin, connect to Asyntai, and the chatbot appears on every page automatically. No theme edits, no JavaScript snippets.


## Installation

1. Go to **Site administration → Plugins → Install Plugins**
2. Upload the ZIP file → **Install Plugin from the ZIP File**
3. Go to **Site administration → Plugins → Local plugins → Asyntai**
4. Click **"Get started"** and complete the connection.
5. The chatbot is enabled on all pages automatically.
6. Customize your chatbot at the [Asyntai Dashboard](https://asyntai.com/dashboard#setup).

Don't have an Asyntai account yet? Create one for free at **[Asyntai.com](https://asyntai.com/auth)**.

**Note: Alternatively, if you don't want to use a plugin, you can also get a JavaScript snippet at [Asyntai.com/dashboard](https://asyntai.com/dashboard) and manually insert it into your web pages.**


## Course content (optional)

By default the chatbot answers from what you set up in the Asyntai dashboard. You can also let it answer from the material inside your own courses.

1. Go to **Site administration → Plugins → Local plugins → Asyntai**
2. Under **Course content**, tick **Let Asyntai read the ticked courses**
3. Tick the courses you want, and the activity types you want
4. Click **Save content settings**

Nothing is read until you tick a course. You can untick a course at any time and it stops being read.

**What is read:** course names, descriptions, pages, book chapters, labels, activity descriptions, and the names of attached files.

**What is never read:** anything belonging to a student. Grades, submissions, forum posts, quiz answers and personal details all stay inside Moodle.

Turning this on switches on Moodle web services and the REST protocol, and creates one token. That token belongs to a service holding a single read-only function, so it cannot reach anything else in Moodle. Disconnecting the plugin deletes the token.
